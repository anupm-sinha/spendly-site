Privacy Policy

Toolpup for Android · published by Appricity

Effective: 4 October 2026

The short version

Toolpup is a collection of everyday tools: to-dos, expenses, trackers, calculators, games and more. It is built to work on your phone. You never need an account, there is no Toolpup server, and there is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDK.

An account is optional and only says who you are. A few tools use the internet when you use them (weather, currency rates, news, translation, holidays, wallpapers), and the Claude assistant is off until you turn it on with your own key. Each is described below.

1. What stays on your phone

Everything you enter — lists, notes, expenses, health logs, documents, photos you attach, your profile, settings — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Toolpup never uploads it anywhere unless you use one of the features below.

Backups. A backup is a file handed to the Android share sheet (or copied to the clipboard); where it goes (Drive, email, another app) is your choice. Your Claude API key, the files in Doc Vault and the text recognised in them are never included in a backup.

On-device intelligence. Search ("Ask Toolpup"), quick-add parsing, insights, receipt reading, document text recognition, expiry-date detection and QR/barcode scanning run on your phone. Camera images used for scanning are processed on the device and are kept only if you save them (for example, a scanned document in Doc Vault).

2. Features that use the internet

These features send the minimum needed to the service named, only when you use them:

  • Weather — Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com, geocoding-api.open-meteo.com): a city name you type, or your approximate location if you allow it.
  • Currency converter — ExchangeRate-API (api.exchangerate-api.com): the base currency.
  • News reader — Google News RSS (news.google.com): the category, search words and region you pick.
  • Translate — MyMemory (api.mymemory.translated.net) and LibreTranslate: the text you ask to translate and the language pair.
  • Scanning models — Google Play services: a one-time download of Google's text and barcode readers; your images never leave the phone.
  • Holidays — Nager.Date (date.nager.at): the country and year.
  • Wallpapers — Lorem Picsum (picsum.photos): image requests.
  • Voice input — your phone's speech recogniser (usually Google): your speech is handled by that app under its own policy; Toolpup receives only the text.
  • Claude assistant (optional) — Anthropic (api.anthropic.com): see section 4.
  • Optional account — Google Firebase Authentication: see section 3.

These services receive your IP address as part of any internet request. Toolpup adds no identifiers of its own.

3. Optional account (only if you sign in)

You never need an account to use Toolpup, and nothing in the app is locked behind one. If you choose to sign in (Settings → Account, or My Profile):

  • Sign-in is provided by Google Firebase Authentication. Depending on the method you pick, Firebase stores your email address (email or Google sign-in), your phone number (phone sign-in), the name on your Google account (Google sign-in), and a user id Firebase creates. Passwords are handled by Firebase; Toolpup never sees or stores them. Like any sign-in service, Firebase also processes your IP address and basic device information to keep sign-in secure.
  • Phone sign-in sends a one-time code to your number by SMS. Before sending it, Firebase checks that the app is genuine using Google Play Integrity (or, failing that, a reCAPTCHA).
  • Google sign-in uses your phone's Google account picker. Toolpup receives your name and email; it does not download your profile photo.
  • The account is identity only. Your name and email or number can appear on your profile in the app. No tool data — notes, expenses, health logs, documents, backups — is sent to Firebase or linked to the account.
  • On the phone, Firebase keeps your signed-in session. Account details are never put in a backup, and the Claude assistant cannot read them.
  • Toolpup and Spendly, both published by Appricity, share the same sign-in service, so one Appricity account works in both.
  • Google processes this data under its own terms: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Deleting your account. In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm. This deletes your account and the details above from Firebase straight away (for safety, Firebase may ask you to sign in again first). Everything on your phone stays exactly as it is. Sign out (same place) ends the session on this phone without deleting the account. If you no longer have the app, see Delete your account.

4. Claude assistant (optional, off by default)

If you turn on the Claude assistant and enter your own Anthropic API key:

  • Your key is stored in Android's encrypted storage on the phone and sent only to Anthropic.
  • When you send a message, Toolpup sends Anthropic your message and the conversation so far. To answer, the assistant may ask the app for specific data (for example "September's expenses" or "today's to-dos"). The app reads that data on your phone and sends it to Anthropic as part of the conversation. The screen shows every such data lookup.
  • It can never read saved passwords, Doc Vault, locked notes, your profile or your account.
  • Nothing is sent until you send a message. The conversation is kept only while the assistant screen is open.
  • Anthropic processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy), using your API account.

Turn the assistant off or delete the key at any time in Settings → Claude assistant.

5. Permissions

  • Internet, network state — the features in section 2, and the optional sign-in.
  • Notifications, run at startup — reminders you set, and re-arming them after a restart.
  • Vibrate — haptic feedback.
  • Approximate location (optional) — local weather, only if you allow it.
  • Camera (optional) — scanning receipts, documents and QR codes, and photos you attach.

Toolpup does not request microphone access; voice input is handled by your phone's speech app.

6. Children

Toolpup is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children under 13.
