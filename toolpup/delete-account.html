Delete your account

Toolpup for Android · published by Appricity

You never needed an account to use Toolpup, and you don't need one to keep using it. Deleting your account removes your sign-in. Everything on your phone stays exactly as it is.

In the app

  1. Open Settings (the gear icon on Home).
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If your sign-in is more than a few minutes old you will be asked to sign in again first — this is a safety check, and you are brought straight back.

You need a connection for this, because the deletion has to be confirmed by the server. If you are offline the app says so and nothing is changed.

Without the app

If you have already uninstalled Toolpup, email appricity.support@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, or tell us the phone number you used, and we will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in identity — the email address, phone number or Google identifier you used, the name from Google, and the user id behind it.

It is removed immediately. Toolpup keeps no other data about you on any server: there are no analytics, no activity logs and no cloud copy of your tools.

Toolpup and Spendly share one Appricity sign-in, so deleting the account signs you out of Spendly too; Spendly's own cloud data is covered by Spendly's deletion page.

What is kept

  • Everything on your device. Your lists, notes, expenses, documents and profile stay on your phone, and Toolpup keeps working without an account.

To remove the data on the device as well, use Settings → Reset All Data, or uninstall the app.

Full details are in the privacy policy, section 3.
