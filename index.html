Spendly

Track every rupee, dollar and yen — entirely offline.

No account. No sign-up. No ads. Most expense apps want your bank login, your email address and a subscription. Spendly wants none of it — open the app and start tracking. Everything stays on your device.

What it does

  • Track everything — expenses, income and transfers, with tags, merchants, receipt photos and split categories
  • Capture without typing — share a bank SMS to Spendly, or scan a paper receipt or payment screenshot. Read on your phone, never uploaded, and always shown to you as a draft to confirm
  • Quick add — type "coffee 4.50" and Spendly works out the rest
  • Budgets that help — monthly and per-category limits, a daily safe-to-spend allowance, rollover, and savings goals
  • Real insights — category breakdowns, six-month trends, top merchants, unusual-spending alerts and net worth over time
  • Accounts & automation — live balances, transfers between your own accounts, and recurring bills added when due
  • Yours to keep — CSV export, JSON backup, PDF reports, and import back any time

Privacy is the design, not a feature

Spendly has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. This version ships without internet permission at all, so it is not merely promising to keep your data private — it is technically incapable of sending it anywhere.

It also cannot read your messages, your notifications or your photo library, and asks for no permission to. When you share a bank alert or scan a receipt, Spendly sees only that one item, reads it on your phone, and shows you a draft to confirm.

Your transactions live in a database on your phone. We never see them. There is nothing to delete from our servers, because there are no servers.
