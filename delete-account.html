Delete your account

Spendly for Android and iOS · published by Appricity

You never needed an account to use Spendly, and you don't need one to keep using it. Deleting your account removes your sign-in and the encrypted copy of your data on our servers. Everything on your phone stays exactly as it is.

In the app

  1. Open Settings (the gear icon).
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If your sign-in is more than a few minutes old you will be asked to sign in again first — this is a safety check, and you are brought straight back.
  4. Confirm once more. That's it.

You need a connection for this, because the deletion has to be confirmed by the server before the app will tell you it is done. If you are offline the app says so and nothing is changed.

Without the app

If you have already uninstalled Spendly, email appricity.support@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, or tell us the phone number you used, and we will delete the account and its cloud copy for you. We will confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in identity — the email address, phone number or Google identifier you used, and the user id behind it.
  • The encrypted cloud copy of your records and the key material used to protect it, if you ever turned sync on.

Both are removed immediately. Nothing is retained, and there is no waiting period: we hold no analytics, no logs of your activity and no backups of the cloud copy.

What is kept

  • Everything on your device. Your transactions, budgets, accounts and receipts are yours and stay on your phone. Spendly keeps working without an account.

To remove the data on the device as well, use Settings → Erase all data, or uninstall the app.

Deleting only the cloud copy

You can keep your account and remove just the cloud copy: Settings → Turn off sync → "Turn off and delete".

Full details are in the privacy policy, section 8.
